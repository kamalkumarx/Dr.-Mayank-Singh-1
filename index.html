About the clinician
A specialist-led approach

Care built around diagnosis, planning and precision.

Dr. Mayank Singh is a BDS, MDS clinician specialising in Periodontology and Implantology. His clinical work is centred around preserving oral health, rebuilding function and creating considered treatment plans for patients requiring advanced dental care.

With experience in 1000+ dental implant placements, he is familiar with single-tooth implants, full-mouth rehabilitation and All-on-X treatment concepts. His postgraduate periodontal training supports a comprehensive approach to gum health, soft-tissue management and the tissues surrounding teeth and implants.

His clinical scope also includes periodontal laser procedures, flap surgeries, extractions, impacted third-molar surgery and selected cyst procedures.

“Every complex case begins with careful diagnosis, thoughtful planning and a treatment approach designed around the patient.”
